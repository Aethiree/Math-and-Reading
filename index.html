Multiply Lab

What does × really mean? ⚡

1
3 × 4 means 3 groups of 4.
🍎🍎🍎🍎4
🍎🍎🍎🍎8
🍎🍎🍎🍎12
Total: 12
2
Tap each group to count it. Watch it skip-count: 4… 8… 12!
3
So 3 × 4 = 12. Multiplying is just fast adding! 🚀
4
You'll also see dot rows and a hopping kangaroo 🦘 — three ways to see the same thing.

Share It!

Division is just fair sharing 🍪

1
12 ÷ 3 means: share 12 cookies onto 3 plates, fairly.
🍪🍪🍪🍪
🍪🍪🍪🍪
🍪🍪🍪🍪
2
Tap Share one to each 👐 — one cookie goes to every plate, again and again.
3
When the jar is empty, count ONE plate. That's the answer: 4!
4
And that's why 3 × 4 = 12. Multiply and divide are opposites! 🔄

AI Academy

What is AI? 🧠

AI means Artificial Intelligence — a computer that learns from examples, instead of being told every rule.

Show it lots of cat pictures, and it learns what a cat looks like. It's a super pattern-spotter! But it can only learn from what you show it — so it can make mistakes.
